Privacy policy

Effective 13 Sept 2026. Short version: handle + progress only. No trackers, no ads, no third-party embeds, no analytics.

What we collect

  • Handle you type at signup. No email, no real name, no phone.
  • Lesson completions, XP, coins, ELO, battles, flags, guild membership, daily wins.
  • One session cookie to keep you logged in. Consent choice in your browser's local storage.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics beacons, no ad pixels, no social widgets, no external fonts or CDNs. Pages load only from this server.
  • No terminal keystroke logging beyond the command you submit to the practice box in that request.
  • No payments, so no card data.

Why and where

To run the skill tree, ranks, and hints. Data lives in the app database (SQLite file on the host). No sale, no sharing, no profiling.

Your rights (PH Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Access, correct, or delete anytime: Profile page has a one-click delete. That wipes handle, progress, badges, flags. For help, file a GitHub issue on Makusu10/CipherHacks and we respond from there — no email collection needed.

Children

Built for learners 16+. Younger users need a guardian to create the handle, or don't use it.

Changes

If paid tiers or real sandboxes ever ship, this page updates first with what changed and why. No silent expansion.
