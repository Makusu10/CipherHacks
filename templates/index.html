The six bands, from hygiene to boss fight

Level unlocks material, not just titles — each band runs roughly twice as deep as the last, and every door needs XP plus the previous checkpoint. Concrete examples under each band so you know what Tuesday night looks like.

  1. 01

    Recruit · Lv 1–10

    Passwords, phishing, CIA triad. Quiz and cards only — no terminal yet. Example: tell which sender is forged, registrar@myschooI.edu.ph, then pass the checkpoint to earn shell access.

  2. 02

    Operator · Lv 11–25

    OSI, ports, Linux, permissions, tiny scripts. Terminal goes live, tightly guided. Example: ls -la finds .note, then cat /home/recruit/flag.txt banks your first flag.

  3. 03

    Analyst · Lv 26–45

    OSINT, scan reading, SQLi, XSS, CSRF on our demo app. Example: scan 10.13.0.5 shows 80/tcp open, probe --web exposes the glued-string login. Hints cost 1.5x now.

  4. 04

    Specialist · Lv 46–70

    Privilege escalation, hash drills, a caged stack overflow (offset only, no shellcode), AD map-reading, wireless defense. Example: sudo -l plus a world-writable cron script — config before cleverness. Hints 2x. This is the wall rote memorization cannot climb.

  5. 05

    Red Team · Lv 71–90

    MITRE-style chains, SSRF and deserialization on a fake fetcher, sandboxed malware report-reading, fake-cloud misconfigs. Example: list-buckets finds a world-readable invoice bucket; you quote the policy line, fix it, move on. Hints 2.5x.

  6. 06

    Apex · Lv 91–100

    Timed boss battle chaining every band plus blue-team log triage — contain first, then cure. Example: a 02:14 login, fresh cron persistence, and an outbound spike means isolate before wiping. Prestige resets you to 1 with a badge and a monthly rotator pool.

Frequently asked questions

Is CipherHacks really free?

Yes. Cards, exams, lessons, and all 8 terminal boxes work with no account. A free handle only saves XP, flags, ELO, and guilds. No card is collected anywhere in this build.

Do I install anything to practice?

No. The terminal, cards, and exams run in your browser against our scripted boxes. To host it yourself you need only Python 3.11+ — the README walks through it in five minutes.

Will this get me hired or certified?

No, and don't trust any trainer that promises that. This builds applied habits — reading scans, Linux fluency, web-bug fixes, log triage — that make later cert study and interviews easier. Badges here are not credentials.

Am I learning to hack real websites?

No. Every technique is taught against fictional systems we ship, with the fix taught beside the bug. Trying these on real networks breaks the code of conduct and Philippine law (RA 10175) — the Terms say so plainly.

How do levels, checkpoints, and Prestige work?

XP sets your level; each band door also needs the previous checkpoint quiz at 70%, so easy-quiz grinding can't skip ahead. At level 100 you may Prestige: reset to 1 with a permanent badge plus a harder monthly challenge pool.

What does CipherHacks store about me?

Your handle plus progress — nothing else. No email, no analytics. One session cookie keeps you logged in, and Profile has a one-click delete that wipes everything. Details live on the Privacy page.
